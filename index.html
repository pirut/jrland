Frontier Survival

A minimalist survival sandbox.

Move: WASD or Left Click to walk
Sprint: Shift
Interact / Gather / Attack: Right Click
Use Item: R
Build Mode: B
Place Building: Left Click (in build mode)
Select Build: Click Build Catalog or Hotbar
Rotate Blueprint: Q
Hotbar: 1-9, Mouse Wheel, or Click
Inventory / Crafting: I
Advanced crafting requires a workbench nearby
Chat: T
Toggle Fullscreen: F (Esc exits)
Pause: P
Toggle Debug: `

Paused

Press P to resume.
